"Always relay through this server" is checked by default, since a direct connection is commonly blocked by a VPN or firewall — relaying connects reliably every time, through your own DropCode server. Uncheck it on a device to attempt a direct peer-to-peer connection first instead (faster, and this server never sees the data), with an automatic fallback to relaying if that doesn't connect within a few seconds.
